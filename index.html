The SEO playbook

SEO, explained without the jargon.

Search optimization helps search engines understand your pages and helps customers find relevant answers. It is a long-term process—not a switch, and never a guaranteed ranking.

Check my opportunities ↓
01

Start with search intent

Learn what potential customers are trying to solve before choosing keywords or writing a page.

02

Choose realistic keywords

Prioritize relevant terms that match your services, locations, and the questions buyers actually ask.

03

Make each page useful

Use clear titles, descriptive headings, helpful copy, internal links, and a visible next step.

04

Strengthen local signals

Keep your business details accurate, complete your Google Business Profile, and build location-relevant service pages where useful.

05

Publish answers customers need

Create genuinely useful guides, FAQs, comparisons, and service information—not pages written only to repeat keywords.

06

Measure what matters

Monitor organic clicks, impressions, qualified calls, form submissions, and conversions. Use the data to improve.
